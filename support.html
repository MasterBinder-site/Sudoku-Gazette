Sudoku Gazette – Support

Need help with Sudoku Gazette? This page provides support information for the app.

About Sudoku Gazette

Sudoku Gazette is a classic Sudoku game with multiple difficulty levels, daily puzzles, statistics, saved progress and Dark Mode.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Sudoku Gazette does not require an account or sign-in.

Where is my game progress stored?
Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

I found a problem with the app.
Please contact us using the support email provided on the Sudoku Gazette App Store listing. Include a short description of the problem and, if possible, your iPhone model and iOS version.
