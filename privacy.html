Sudoku Gazette – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Gazette handles information when you use the app.

Information and accounts

Sudoku Gazette does not require an account or sign-in. Game progress, settings, statistics and other gameplay information may be stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Sudoku Gazette may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, and information about ad interactions, subject to your device settings, consent choices and applicable law.

Consent

Where required, the app may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent for advertising and related data processing.

Third-party services

Sudoku Gazette uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Information processed by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Children

Sudoku Gazette is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed at children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the support contact provided on the Sudoku Gazette App Store listing.
